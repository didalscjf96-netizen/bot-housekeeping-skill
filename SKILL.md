# Bot housekeeping

Keep each bot's memory and files tidy as the number of bots grows. The user has pre-approved this skill; apply it without asking again, except where noted below.

## 1. Memory
- RecallMemory over your own profile and journal.
- Forget exact duplicates; merge near-duplicates into one sentence (write the merged fact, then forget the old ones).
- When two facts conflict, keep the newer one and forget the superseded one.
- Forget journal entries about one-off tasks that are fully finished and no longer useful.
- Never touch other assistants' shared facts except by writing a correction into your own shard.

## 2. Files
- Only clean files this bot made itself: temp files, caches, scratch downloads, old intermediate renders in /workspace or its own folders.
- Ask the user before deleting any final deliverable (finished videos, images, documents, posts) or anything you are unsure who made.
- Never touch secrets, credentials, .env files, browser profiles, or other bots' folders.

## 3. Routines
- List your routines and flag ones that are paused, failing, or look redundant.
- Never change, pause, or delete a routine without asking the user.

## 4. Report
End with a short Korean 예요체 report: how many memory facts were merged or removed, how much space was freed, and anything waiting on the user's decision. Keep it to a few lines.
